    A site for everyone

    The City wants every resident to read and use this website, whatever their age, eyesight, hands or connection.

    What we promise Make it easier Tell us How it is checked

    for readers aged 13 to 80

    What we promise

    The site follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, which covers the Philippine Web Accessibility policy (DICT MC 2017-004).

    • Large, clear typeReading text at 18 px, nothing under 14 px, and dark ink on paper for every line you read.
    • A keyboard is enoughEvery link and control can be reached with the Tab key, and you can always see where you are.
    • Written for screen readersHeadings, landmarks and labels name every part of the page; the drawings stay out of the way.
    • Motion you controlPause motion, at the top of every page, stops every moving drawing, and the site remembers it.
    • A layout for phonesHalf the readers are on a phone: a layout of their own, with every tap target 44 px or more.
    • Nothing from elsewhereNo trackers, no adverts, nothing loaded from another website.

    your browser can do more

    Make it easier

    A few settings help, on any page of this site.

    1. Bigger textCtrl and + (Command and + on a Mac) zooms in; the page rearranges itself up to 400%.
    2. Less motionPress Pause motion, or turn on Reduce motion in your phone's or computer's settings.
    3. More contrastWindows contrast themes are followed: the page takes your theme's colours.
    4. Read aloudYour device's screen reader can read every page; tell us where it stumbles.

    How it is checked

    Every page of this prototype, at desktop and phone sizes, before each change is kept.

    • An automated auditaxe-core against WCAG 2.2 AA, on every page.
    • A readability auditType size and contrast measured on the real pixels, at eleven screen sizes.
    • By handA keyboard walk, reduced motion, no scripts, 400% zoom and contrast themes.
    • Still to doTests with screen readers and with residents, before launch. To be done
