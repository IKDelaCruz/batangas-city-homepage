    Citizen's Charter 2026 · Civil Registrar

    Copy of a birth, marriage or death certificate

    For anyone born or married in Batangas City, and the next of kin of someone who died here.

    At a glance

    Office
    City Civil Registrar, Records Division
    Classification
    Simple
    For
    Citizens (G2C)
    Fee
    ₱80.00 a copy
    Total time
    2 hours 24 minutes
    Where
    Public assistance desk, ground floor, Civil Registrar's building

    the checklist

    What to bring

    And where to get each one.

    Requirements and where to secure them
    RequirementWhere to get it
    The request form, filled in and signed at the back, one originalThe public assistance desk, ground floor
    The City's official receipt, originalThe cashier, Office of the City Treasurer
    A valid ID, the original and one photocopyThe agency that issued it
    For someone else's record: an authorisation letter, special power of attorney, or affidavit of kinship or guardianshipThe person concerned
    The record from the Philippine Statistics Authority, one originalThe PSA

    yours, and the office's

    Step by step

    Who does what, what it costs, how long it takes, and who answers for it.

    Steps, fees, processing time and the person responsible
    StepYouThe officeFeeTimeResponsible

    2Pay the feeIssues the official receipt₱80.00 a copy15 minutesRevenue collection clerk, City Treasurer
