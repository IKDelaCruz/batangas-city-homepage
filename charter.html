    How long, at most

    Every service is classified, and the law sets the longest it may take, in working days.

    • 3days for a simple service
    • 7days for a complex one
    • 20days for a highly technical one
    • What to bringand where to get each paper
    • The stepsyours, and the office's
    • The feesnothing beyond what is listed
    • Who answersa person responsible for each step

    When it goes wrong

    Tell the office first; if it is not resolved, the national hotline and the Anti-Red Tape Authority.

    1. The officeits public assistance desk
    2. City Hallthe feedback form
    3. 8888the national hotline
    4. ARTAarta.gov.ph (opens in a new tab)
