    Built on heritage

    A town called Batangan, a church at its centre, and more than four centuries since.

    1. 1581Batangan is founded, with its first church; the name comes from the big logs, batang, plentiful in its river.
    2. 1857The present church is finished, six years after its first stone.
    3. 1948It is declared a minor basilica.
    4. 1969Republic Act 5495, approved 21 June, makes Batangas a city; its government begins on 23 July, now Foundation Day.

    between the dates

    How the town grew

    A church, a name, a school, a plaza.

    1. 1601Batangan becomes Batangas
    2. 1901Civil government begins, 4 July
    3. 1909The Municipal Hall opens, 19 June
    4. 1910A trade school, now Batangas State University
    5. 1915Plaza Mabini opens, 25 July
    6. 2022La Batangueña, the Basilica's image, granted a papal coronation

    The Basilica

    Across Plaza Mabini from City Hall. Its octagonal belfry holds the baptistery at its base; the façade, brought down by the 1942 earthquake, was rebuilt in 1946.

    • 1851first stone
    • 1857blessed, 2 February
    • 1948a minor basilica, 13 February

    The Sublian Festival

    Half prayer, half dance. Begun in 1988, the festival runs two weeks and ends on 23 July, the city's foundation day.
