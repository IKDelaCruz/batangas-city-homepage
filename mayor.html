    From City Hall to Congress, and back

    Mario Vittorio Agregado Mariño, called Marvey.

    1. Before 2016City Councilor, then Board Member of the Batangas Provincial Board
    2. 2016The 5th District's first Representative
    3. 2019, 2022Re-elected; nine years in Congress
    4. 2025Elected Mayor, sworn in 30 June at the People's Quadrangle
    • 116,487votes in May 2025, proclaimed 13 May
    • 9years as Representative of Batangas City
    • 2025NDCP Alumni Association Outstanding Alumni Award

    His work in Congress

    Two committees chaired, and a law for the city's own day.

    • Republic Act 11377His law, 2019: every 23 July a special working holiday for Batangas City's foundation day.
    • Civil ServiceChairman, Committee on Civil Service and Professional Regulation, from 2017.
    • Trade and IndustryChairman of the committee in the 19th Congress.
