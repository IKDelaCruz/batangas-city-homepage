    About EBD and the MACOne desk for every social programme

    A resident, a family or a barangay brings a need to one desk, and the desk finds the programme. The programmes carry the initials of former Mayor Eduardo B. Dimacuha, who began them; Mayor Marvey A. Mariño has said they will be continued and widened. To be confirmed

    Mayor's Action CenterCity Hall Complex, P. Burgos Street, Barangay 17. Hotline 0999-222-6626, office (043) 786-1920.

    EBD Health CardWhat to bring, and help beyond the card

    What to bring

    • A valid ID and proof you live in the city
    • A medical certificate or abstract
    • The hospital's statement of account
    • A certificate for medical assistance from your barangay

    Partner hospitals, since October 2025

    • Batangas Medical Center
    • St. Patrick's
    • St. Camillus de Lellis
    • Jesus of Nazareth
    • Health Care Specialist
    • Golden Gate

    For a bill the card does not cover, the MAC issues an EBD one-time endorsement or a DOH guarantee letter: hospital bills, laboratory fees, chemotherapy, anti-rabies vaccines. About 200 beds are kept for cardholders.

    Apply for the card (opens in a new tab)
    EBD ScholarshipWho qualifies and how to apply

    Who qualifies

    • Five years a resident of Batangas City
    • An 85% average in high school, or a 2.5 GWA in college
    • Family income of ₱400,000 or less
    • Parents, and the applicant if 18 or over, registered voters of the city
    1. Applyto the MAC Scholarship Committee
    2. Get listedschedule in the advisories
    3. Receiveon a Philippine Veterans Bank debit card

    For the first semester of school year 2025–26 the City paid ₱5,000 to each of some 6,000 college scholars.

    Top three Grade 12 graduates: the EBD-MAM scholarship at the Colegio ng Lungsod ng Batangas, free tuition and ₱7,000 a semester.

    Apply for the scholarship (opens in a new tab)

    Certificates

    City Hall, ground floor

    Birth, marriage and death certificatesA copy from the City, or on PSA paper

    What to bring

    • A valid ID
    • Name, date and place on the record
    • For someone else's record, their authorisation

    Agencies and embassies usually ask for the PSA copy, ordered online or at a PSA outlet.

    Marriage licencePosted ten days, valid 120 days

    What to bring

    • Birth certificates on PSA paper
    • A CENOMAR for each of you
    • Valid IDs
    • The pre-marriage counselling certificate
    • Parents' consent (18 to 21) or advice (21 to 25)
    Community tax certificateThe cedula, once a year

    What to bring

    • A valid ID and your address
    • Your income and property, declared at the counter

    City TreasurerCity Hall, and barangay halls for their residents.

    Certified true copiesRecords, ordinances, tax declarations

    What to bring

    • A valid ID
    • The names, dates or numbers on the document

    Ask the office that keeps it: the Civil Registrar, the Council Secretariat or the City Assessor.

    Permits

    before you build or open

    Business permitNew, or renewed within January
    1. RegisterDTI or SEC
    2. Barangaybusiness clearance
    3. Fileat the BPLO
    4. Clearanceszoning, fire, sanitary
    5. Payat the Treasurer
    6. Claimpermit and plate

    Renew from 1 to 31 January. After that, a 25% surcharge and 2% interest a month.

    Building permitBefore you build, add or renovate

    What to bring

    • Signed and sealed plans
    • Title or tax declaration, and lot plan
    • Zoning clearance
    • Fire safety evaluation clearance
    • Bill of materials

    • Fire safety inspection certificate
    • The building permit and logbook

    Social services

    under the covered court

    Senior citizen IDAged 60 and over, free

    What to bring

    • Proof of age
    • Proof of residence
    • Two 1×1 photos

    Issued by the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs. The 20% discount and VAT exemption of the Expanded Senior Citizens Act.

    PWD IDFor residents with a disability, free

    What to bring

    • A medical certificate of the disability
    • Barangay certificate of residence
    • Valid ID and two photos

    What to bring

    • The children's birth certificates
    • Proof of being a solo parent
    • Barangay certificate, ID and photos

    Under the Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act, RA 11861; valid one year, renewed at the City Social Welfare office.
