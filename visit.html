    p. 01 Heritage

    A plaza, a basilica, four centuries.

    Plaza Mabini sits at the heart of the Poblacion, a short walk from the Basilica of the Immaculate Conception. The church you see today was finished in 1857.

    Walk the old town

    p. 02 Food

    Strong coffee, slow broth.

    Barako coffee, brewed dark and poured early. Bulalo beef soup, on the fire since morning. Batangas does not cook in a hurry.

    Where to eat

    p. 03 Culture

    The subli, half prayer, half dance.

    An old Batangueño devotion in movement, danced with hats, bamboo and drum. The Sublian Festival keeps it alive every July, two weeks that end on 23 July, the city's foundation day.

    Culture and the arts

    p. 04 The bay

    Where the bay meets the passage.

    Batangas Bay opens onto the Verde Island Passage, one of the richest stretches of sea anywhere. Verde Island, part of the city, sits in the middle of it.

    See it on the map

    p. 05 Festivals

    The river carries the feast.

    Each January the image of the Sto. Niño travels the Calumpang by boat, and the city lines the banks and the bridge to meet it.

    The fiesta this year

    p. 06 Destinations

    Up to Montemaria.

    On a headland in Pagkilatan, the Mother of All Asia, Tower of Peace, looks out over the passage toward Mindoro.

    Plan a visit

    by road or by sea

    Plan a visit

    About two hours from Manila, outside the rush.

    • By roadSLEX, then the STAR Tollway to the city. Buses to the Grand Terminal and the port.
    • By seaFerries from Mindoro, Romblon and the Visayas into the international port.
    • Getting aroundJeepneys on the main roads, tricycles for the rest; the Poblacion on foot.

    Where to eat

    Batangas does not cook in a hurry, and most of it is best in the morning.

    • Batangas lomiThick noodles in a dark broth, born in Lipa, eaten across the province.
    • BulaloBatangas beef shank and marrow, on the fire since morning.
    • Kapeng barakoThe strong liberica of the province, poured early.
    • GotoOx tripe and offal in a clear broth, without rice.
    • Sinaing na tulinganBullet tuna slow-cooked in clay.
    • Adobo sa dilawFrom Taal, yellow with turmeric.
